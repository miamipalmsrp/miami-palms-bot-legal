Privacy Policy

Draft: November 2, 2026

1. Overview

Palm Privilege is a private Discord bot being developed for the Miami Palms RP community. Its intended purpose is to identify eligible Discord priority roles and support the FiveM server's SpoodyQueue priority system.

2. Information Used

The planned integration may need to access:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server membership
  • Assigned Discord roles
  • Role-to-priority tier mappings
  • FiveM player identifiers needed for matching

The exact information processed will be confirmed before the bot becomes operational.

3. How Information Is Used

The intended uses are verifying priority-pass eligibility, identifying the appropriate queue tier, and communicating priority information to the FiveM queue integration.

4. Storage and Retention

The bot is currently under development. Its storage methods, logging practices, and retention periods have not yet been finalized. This section will be updated before the bot is made operational.

5. Information Sharing

The planned system involves Discord and the Miami Palms RP FiveM queue integration. Information needed for queue verification may be processed between these systems. Any additional service providers will be identified before deployment.

6. Data Security

The integration is intended to use access controls and safeguards appropriate to the information it processes. Security arrangements will be reviewed before deployment.

7. Data Requests

Users may contact Miami Palms RP administration through the official Discord server to ask about their information or request deletion of data controlled by Palm Privilege. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Policy Updates

This document is a development-stage draft. It will be updated to describe the bot's actual data processing, storage, retention, and deletion practices before public use.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Miami Palms RP administration team through the official community Discord.
